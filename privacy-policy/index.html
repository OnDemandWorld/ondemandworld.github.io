01

Importance

By using this application, you signify your acceptance of our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to this policy, please do not use this application.

Internet and mobile technologies evolve rapidly, and this Privacy Policy may be amended unilaterally by us from time to time to reflect the situation. We recommend that you regularly check this page to ensure that you have read the most recent version.

02

Preamble

The objective of this Privacy Policy is to establish and further users’ trust and confidence in using this application and by promoting the use of fair information practices.

To make it simple enough for anyone to understand this Privacy Policy, this Privacy Policy was drafted as transparent and non-technical as possible. In brief, this Privacy Policy is mainly about the following:

  • Collection of information (“Information”) about you or third party
  • Use of Information
  • Sharing of Information
  • Security measures that we have taken to protect Information
03

Our Principle

We take privacy issue seriously, and will always respect your privacy. We take all measures available to us to care for any Information that you have given us, and procure that such Information will not be used other than the purposes stated in this Privacy Policy. We have created this Privacy Policy because we believe our name and reputation stem from the trust of our users, and we hope that our commitment to online privacy will make you feel safe and encourage your usage of this application.

04

Collection of Information

Information is collected mainly in the following ways:

1. Information You Actively Provide

You can decide what personal Information you provide to us, and we will only collect such Information that you choose to provide. For example, you may provide us Information when you: (a) register an account with us; (b) list your profile; (c) post contents including but not limited to videos, photos, and private messages; (d) contact us with an enquiry; (e) fill out a survey or other form with your personal Information included; (f) enter a competition; (g) post information such as comments on other users; or (h) participate in a promotion.

2. Information Collected Automatically

To facilitate your usage of this application and to personalise your online experience, we may make use of cookies, web beacons, and other related technologies. Information such as your IP address, the type of mobile device you are using, the date and time of your usage of this application, and your selection of restaurant.

3. Third Parties

We may allow third-parties, including our authorized service providers, to display advertisements on our application. These companies may use tracking technologies, such as cookies, to collect information about users who view or interact with their advertisements. Our application does not provide any personal Information to these third parties. Such Information allows them to deliver targeted advertisements and gauge their effectiveness.

4. Location Data

In order to deliver excellent services, such as providing search result most relevant to your needs, we may receive information about your actual location (such as GPS signals sent by a mobile device) or information that can be used to approximate a location (such as a cell ID).

Wherever the users are located, the users are responsible to ensure that the contents provided by him/her conform to all local data protection laws. We are not responsible for the contents provided by the user.

05

Use of Information

Information is used mainly in the following ways:

1. Marketing Purposes

We may use Information for internal marketing analysis. For example, we may use the Information to assess trends amongst our consumers or to measure the amount of traffic to our application. We may use your personal Information to tell you about our products and services and those of our group companies and any of their related businesses via app notification, email, or other means. We may also share non-personal Information with others, such as advertisers, in aggregate anonymous form, which means that the Information will not contain any personally identifiable information about you.

2. Third Party Offers

We may arrange for carefully selected organisations to send you marketing and promotional information that may be of interest to you. In such circumstances your personal information may be disclosed to these organisations who will agree to be bound by the terms of this Privacy Policy.

3. Administration and Data Management

We may transfer your personal Information to third parties under confidentiality obligations when the performance of any service in relation to the activities above is sub-contracted.

4. Location Data

We provide relevant and helpful information based on where you are. If you tell us where you are (i.e., by allowing your mobile device to send us your location), we may use that information to provide you with location-based information. If you want to deactivate this feature, you can disable location services on your mobile device.

06

Sharing of Information

Information may be shared mainly in the following circumstances:

1. Collaboration with Business Partners

We may share personal Information and non-personal Information with our business partners who assist us by performing core services (such as website hosting, data storage and security) related to our operation of our application and/or by making certain services available to our users. Those business partners shall be bound to uphold the same standards of security and confidentiality that we have promised to you in this Privacy Policy.

2. Sale and Transfer

In the event of a sale, merger, consolidation, change in control, transfer of substantial assets, reorganisation or liquidation, we may transfer, sell, or assign to third parties Information concerning your relationship with us, including without limitation, personally identifiable Information that you provide and other information concerning your relationship with us.

3. Legal Purposes

We may disclose personal information if required to do so by law or in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to conform with the law or legal process or to protect or defend our rights or property.

07

Security of Information

We take appropriate security measures (including physical, electronic and procedural measures) to help safeguard your personal Information from unauthorized access and disclosure. We also want you to feel confident using our services. Nevertheless, no system can be completely secure. Therefore, although we take steps to secure your Information, we do not promise, and you should not expect, that your personal information, searches, or other communications will always remain secure. Users should also take care with how they handle and disclose their personal Information and should avoid sending personal Information through insecure network.

08

Third Party Websites

We may provide links to third party websites as a service to our users. Please be aware that we cannot control and are not responsible for the information collection practices of such websites, which may differ from those of us. You are advised to review and understand the privacy policies on these websites before providing any information to them.

09

Questions

Protecting your privacy is one of our highest priorities. If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy, you may email us at support@ondemandworld.com.
