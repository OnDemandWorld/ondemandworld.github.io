About OnDemandWorld

Imagine a world which links over one-third of us - both with each other and our physical environment. From the humble smartphone to ubiquitous sensors which monitor, predict and intervene in our health, our supply chains, our dating habits, our interests, our buying patterns... our everything...

Imagine a world where technology that is at once simplified and sophisticated is distributed to populations and communities that could never access it before. As people learn and connect in ways that take the old generation by surprise, what new companies or industries will be formed? Which brilliant individuals who would otherwise have not met their potential will emerge?

We’re a web & mobile software company that understands technology is changing our world like never before and wants to play a part.

Our focus for the last five years has been on building technology frameworks that power simple, elegant apps on smart mobile devices. With 20 employees and offices in London (founded June ’08) and Beijing (founded Feb ’10), we have deep skill-sets in all aspects of mobile and web applications from development to promotion. We’re privately-owned, profitable and have angel-funding available for future expansion.

Just as importantly, we’re fascinated by what will happen as the next billion people access the internet in new ways and are perfectly positioned to play a role in whatever those people do next...
