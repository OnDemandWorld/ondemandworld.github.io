House Rules

We want you to feel as confident and as comfortable as possible whenever you are using any of our apps and services. A few plain-language rules keep the platform healthy for everyone.

Home → House Rules
01

Why these rules exist

We want you to feel as confident and as comfortable as possible whenever you are using any of our app and services. The way software services can be used changes extremely quickly, so it is important for us to highlight a few house rules for our users.

02

Our side of the deal

From our side, you need to know that we do not sell your data — it’s not part of our business model. In fact, we try hard to store as little of your personal information as we can; it’s less risky for us. But we want you to know that information we ask is for providing a better user experience or for us to contact you. We mainly use in-app or system notification, but emails are still useful for password resets and other support purposes.

03

A community platform

This platform is designed to let you interact and meet thousands of like-minded people sharing similar interests. The platform allows people to post and share photos and messages, chat, and share experience etc.

To maintain the platform, we keep the contents relevant for you, and will periodically moderate the content published on our platform.

04

What we remove

We will remove any third-party content on our platforms that contains any of the following:

  • Discrimination of any kind: sexist, ageist, racist etc.
  • Spam: including commercial nature.
  • Any content which is infringing, obscene, indecent, misleading or unlawful.
  • Any content which creates privacy or security risk to anyone.
  • Any activity which in our sole discretion deems to be inappropriate or immoral.

It is very rare that we need to enforce these rules, but we hope you understand the reasons.

05

Changes & your content

We reserve the right to change these terms and conditions at any point, not least because things change so fast in the online environment. If they are important to you, please check back regularly.

By sharing images, video, or other material on this platform, you grant permission for us to use your content in our marketing material.

Please understand, we cannot be held responsible for any content posted by our community. If you do not agree to any of these House Rules, the Terms of Use, and Privacy Policy, do not use our platform.

The short version Be decent, don't spam, don't put anyone at risk. We keep your data minimal and we never sell it. Everything else is in the Terms and Privacy Policy.
