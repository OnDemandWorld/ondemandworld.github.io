01

Who we are

Established in 2009, coinciding with the initial release of iOS, we have been dedicated to crafting exceptional mobile applications ever since. Primarily focusing on iOS platforms, we have also successfully ported key applications to Android. Our expertise encompasses a diverse range of app genres, including Geolocation, Travel Guides, Social Networking, and Chinese Language Study apps.

Today our flagship work is ODW.ai — ten focused, open-source AI modules that share one identity, one data boundary, and one audit trail. Free to self-host, built in the open, sovereign by design. The same team that shipped consumer apps for a decade and institutional blockchain systems now builds the operational AI layer for small and medium businesses.

The journey so far

We venture into Bitcoin mining, exploring the possibilities within this innovative field.

Blockchain + machine learning

We integrate blockchain technology and machine learning into some of our applications — the foundation for everything that follows.

Remote-first

Remote work becomes the norm for our team, with the exception of those based in Hong Kong or Shenzhen.

AssetTech · Niftnack · TechHub Jobs

Three blockchain products ship: AssetTech (ERC-1400 security-token platform), Niftnack (NFT platform for digital assets, memberships and loyalty points), and TechHub Jobs (recruitment platform with tokenomics). See Our Products.

Now

ODW.ai — open-source operational AI

Our AI solutions live on ODW.ai. The AssetTech stack has been open-sourced as a reference implementation, and most of our legacy mobile apps are being released on GitHub as open source.

03

Open source is the default

We are releasing most of our previous mobile apps on GitHub as open source — free to copy the code. We keep all our branding and media assets: only the source code is given away, none of the content or other IP. All non-source-code copyrights are retained, including but not limited to branding, graphics, UI/UX, and marketing.

The ODW.ai suite follows the same philosophy: Apache 2.0 and MIT throughout, free to self-host forever, no seats, no meters, no lock-in. We earn our keep on deployment, customisation, and support — not by holding your data hostage.

Interested in licensing? We have a suite of related domains for many of our legacy apps, available for purchase. See the legacy portfolio or get in touch.
